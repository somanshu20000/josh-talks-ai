RESEARCH March 2026 · Human-1

The world's first
open full-duplex Hindi
conversational AI model.

Human-1 is our duplex conversational speech model — the first model trained to participate in a conversation, not just generate audio from text. It can listen and speak simultaneously, enabling real-time voice interactions that match the natural rhythm of human dialogue.

Unlike turn-based voice systems, Human-1 can acknowledge users mid-sentence, respond in real time, and handle interruptions without breaking conversational flow. Trained on Large-scale hours of natural Hindi conversations — the first milestone toward a one-million-hour conversational corpus.

Evaluation Platform

VoiceArena.

The measurement layer for voice AI. Blind pairwise human evaluation of text-to-speech models — with Bradley-Terry Elo rankings designed for continuous updates.

Evaluators listen, not read. Comment-annotated votes surface qualitative failure modes — robotic tone, wrong pause, accent drift — that Elo scores alone cannot surface. Evaluation interface designed for comparing model outputs.

STEP 01
Same text, two models
Identical input sent to both models independently; each synthesises speech without knowing the other exists.
STEP 02
Blind human evaluation
Evaluator hears both audio outputs without knowing which model produced them. Judges naturalness, not brand.
STEP 03
Vote, comment, Elo update
Evaluator selects the better audio and explains why. Ranking models can aggregate evaluation results.
